Goa Travel Guide

Everything you need to plan your Goa trip.

💰

Estimated Budget

₹5,000–₹10,000 per person for a short budget trip, excluding travel to Goa.

✈️

How to Reach

Goa can be reached by train, bus or flight. Madgaon and Thivim are major railway stations.

🏨

Where to Stay

Choose accommodation near North Goa beaches, Panaji or South Goa depending on your trip.

🍜

Food

Try Goan seafood, fish curry rice, vindaloo, local snacks and refreshing drinks.

🗓️

Best Time

November to February is popular for pleasant weather and beach activities.

⭐

Travel Tip

Carry sunscreen, comfortable clothes and stay hydrated while exploring Goa.
