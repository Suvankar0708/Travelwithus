Darjeeling Travel Guide

Everything you need to plan your Darjeeling trip.

💰

Estimated Budget

₹4,000–₹8,000 per person for a short budget trip, excluding travel to Darjeeling.

🚆

How to Reach

Reach New Jalpaiguri by train and continue to Darjeeling by shared taxi, bus or car.

🏨

Where to Stay

Choose hotels or guesthouses around Darjeeling Mall, Chowrasta or nearby areas.

🍜

Food

Try momos, thukpa, local tea and delicious Tibetan and Bengali dishes.

🗓️

Best Time

March to May and October to December are popular periods for pleasant weather and views.

⭐

Travel Tip

Carry warm clothes and comfortable shoes, especially if you plan to explore the hills.
