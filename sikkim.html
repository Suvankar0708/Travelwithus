Sikkim Travel Guide

Everything you need to plan your Sikkim trip.

💰

Estimated Budget

₹5,000–₹10,000 per person for a short budget trip, excluding travel to Sikkim.

🚆

How to Reach

Reach New Jalpaiguri or Bagdogra and continue to Sikkim by shared taxi, bus or car.

🏨

Where to Stay

Gangtok offers many hotels and guesthouses for different budgets.

🍜

Food

Try momos, thukpa, Tibetan dishes and traditional Sikkimese food.

🗓️

Best Time

March to May and October to December are popular times to explore Sikkim.

⭐

Travel Tip

Carry warm clothes and comfortable shoes. Mountain weather can change quickly.
